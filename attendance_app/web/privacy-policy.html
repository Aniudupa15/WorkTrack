Privacy Policy — PunchIn

Last updated: 3 August 2026

PunchIn ("the app", "we", "us") is a workplace attendance application. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how it is handled. By using PunchIn you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your name and email address, used to create and manage your account.
  • Employment details — your company, department, position, and assigned shift and work location, used to operate attendance.
  • Precise location — captured only when you check in or out, to verify you are within your workplace's geofence.
  • Photos — an optional selfie captured during check-in, if enabled by your organization.
  • Device identifiers — a push-notification token used to deliver notifications to your device.
  • Diagnostics & usage — crash reports and anonymous usage analytics that help us keep the app stable and improve it.

How we use your information

Your information is used solely to provide the app's features: authentication, recording attendance, geofence verification, leave management, reporting, and app stability. We do not use it for advertising.

How your data is stored and processed

Data is stored using Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Analytics, and Crashlytics), which acts as our data processor. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). See Google's Firebase privacy information.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. Data is accessible to your organization's administrator within the app, and to Google as our infrastructure provider.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained while your account is active. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by contacting us at the email below; we will act on verified requests within a reasonable period.

Children

PunchIn is a business tool intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
