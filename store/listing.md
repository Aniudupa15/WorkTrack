## Full description (≤4000)
PunchIn is attendance your team can trust. Staff punch in from their own phone, only when they're actually at work, and you see who's in, who's late and who's on leave — across every site.

BUILT FOR TEAMS THAT WORK AT MANY PLACES
Security agencies, housekeeping and facility teams, field technicians, site crews, shops and offices.

FOR STAFF
• Punch in and out in one tap — the app checks you're inside your work location
• Join with an invite code, QR or SMS link and sign in with your mobile number — no password
• See your month at a glance: present, late, leave
• Request full-day or half-day leave and track approval
• Shift reminders if you haven't punched in

FOR OWNERS AND MANAGERS
• Multiple work locations, each with its own geofence radius
• Live view of who's in today, late marks and absences
• Approve leave requests and new joiners
• Invite by WhatsApp, SMS, link or printed QR poster — or upload your whole team from Excel/CSV
• Reports and payroll export for Tally, Zoho Payroll and Keka (Business)
• Selfie at punch-in for extra proof (Business)

TAMPER-PROOF BY DESIGN
• Punch times come from our server, not the phone clock
• Location is checked only at the moment of punching — never tracked in the background
• Every rule is enforced on the server, not just in the app
