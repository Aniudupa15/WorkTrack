Built for teams that work at many places

Geofenced punch-in

Each work location has its own geofence. Staff can only punch in from inside it, and tries from outside are flagged.

Server time, not phone time

Punch times come from our server, so they can't be backdated by changing the phone clock.

Live view of every site

A live map and list of who's in, who's late and who is absent today, for each location.

Leave and holidays

Full-day and half-day leave requests, yearly allowances, approvals and a company holiday list.

Reports and payroll

Monthly attendance reports and payroll export for Tally, Zoho Payroll and Keka.

Easy onboarding

Invite staff by code, QR poster or WhatsApp, or upload your team from Excel. Staff sign in with their mobile number.

Who uses PunchIn

Security agencies, housekeeping and facility management companies, field service and installation teams, construction and interior site crews, packers and movers, shops and offices.
