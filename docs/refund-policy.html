Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Free trial

New companies get a 14-day free trial. No payment is taken during the trial, so there is nothing to refund. If you don't choose a paid plan, the company moves to the Free plan when the trial ends.

Cancelling a subscription

An administrator can cancel at any time from Company → Plan & billing in the web app, or by emailing aniudupa15@gmail.com. After cancelling, the plan stays active until the end of the period already paid for, and no further payments are taken. The company then moves to the Free plan; its data is kept.

Refunds

  • First payment: if you are not satisfied, request a full refund within 7 days of your first payment.
  • Duplicate or wrong charges: charges made in error are always refunded in full.
  • Apart from the above, payments for a period that has started are not refundable, but you will not be charged again after cancelling.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method (UPI, card or bank account) through Razorpay. Your bank may take a few more days to show the credit.
