Delete your PunchIn account and data

App: PunchIn — Attendance · Developer: Anirudha Udupa

You can request deletion of your PunchIn account and all associated personal data at any time. Follow the steps below.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to aniudupa15@gmail.com from the email address registered to your PunchIn account.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my PunchIn account.
  3. We will verify the request and delete your account and associated data within 30 days, and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile (name, email, department, position, shift, work location)
  • Your attendance records and leave requests
  • Any check-in photos associated with your account

What may be retained

We do not retain personal data after deletion, except where required to comply with a legal obligation. Anonymous, aggregated analytics that cannot identify you may be kept.
